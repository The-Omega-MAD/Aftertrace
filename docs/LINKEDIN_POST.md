**A security alert can look familiar and still be a different incident.**

That’s the problem I built AFTERTRACE to address.

It uses Hindsight to carry investigation context across incidents:

- Retain analyst findings, lessons, and outcomes.
- Recall related investigations when a new alert arrives.
- Compare current evidence with the historical record.
- Highlight what changed instead of inheriting an old verdict.
- Keep the final decision with the human analyst.

The interesting part is the Evidence Delta: same application, new permission event, different investigation.

Hindsight makes the history available. AFTERTRACE makes the difference visible.
